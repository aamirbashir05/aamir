========================================================
              ACCOUNT ROOMS
   Har TikTok account ka apna alag "room"
========================================================


--------------------------------------------------------
1. YE APP HAI KYA?
--------------------------------------------------------

Account Rooms ek Android app hai jo un logon ke liye banai gayi
hai jo TikTok (ya kisi bhi platform) par EK SE ZYADA ACCOUNTS
chalate hain.

Har account ke liye aap ek "room" banate hain. Us room ke andar
us account se juri har cheez ek jagah mehfooz ho jati hai:

   - Account ka naam aur username
   - Kaunsi VPN app chalani hai aur kaunsa server
   - Kaunsi Fake GPS app chalani hai aur kaunsi location
   - TikTok app
   - Notes (niche, posting time, hashtags)
   - Upload log (kis account par kab kya post kiya)

Phir jab bhi us account par kaam karna ho, bas us room ko kholein
aur teen button dabate jayein. Bas.


--------------------------------------------------------
2. YE APP BANI KYUN? (MASLA KYA THA)
--------------------------------------------------------

Jo banda 4-5 accounts chalata hai, uska har baar ka amal ye hota
hai:

   1. Phone me VPN app dhoondo  -> kholo -> sahi server chuno
   2. Wapas home screen         -> Fake GPS app dhoondo
   3. Usme sahi city / coordinates yaad karke daalo
   4. Wapas home screen         -> TikTok kholo
   5. Sahi account par switch karo
   6. Aur agar bhool gaye ke "is account ke liye kaunsi location
      thi?" to phir se sochte raho

Ye har baar 3-5 minute kha jata hai, aur ghalti ka khatra alag —
ek account ki setting doosre account par lag jati hai.

ACCOUNT ROOMS ye poora amal ek screen par le aata hai. Kuch
dhoondna nahi parta, kuch yaad rakhna nahi parta.


--------------------------------------------------------
3. YE APP KYA KARTI HAI (AUR KYA NAHI KARTI)
--------------------------------------------------------

KARTI HAI:
   + Har account ki setting yaad rakhti hai
   + Aapki apni VPN / GPS / TikTok apps ko seedha kholti hai
   + Coordinates khud clipboard par daal deti hai (paste karo, bas)
   + Batati hai ke aap kis step par pahunche hain (1/3, 2/3, 3/3)
   + Har account ka upload record rakhti hai

NAHI KARTI (ye saaf samajh lein):
   - Ye khud VPN NAHI hai. Aapki apni VPN app chahiye.
   - Ye khud location NAHI badalti. Aapki apni GPS app chahiye.
   - Ye TikTok account khud switch NAHI karti. Wo aap TikTok ke
     andar karte hain.
   - Ye app ke andar app NAHI chalati (koi cloning/container nahi).

Yaani ye ek ORGANIZER aur LAUNCHER hai — aapka jo tareeqa pehle
se hai, usi ko tez aur tarteeb-wala bana deti hai.


--------------------------------------------------------
4. ISTEMAAL KA TAREEQA (STEP BY STEP)
--------------------------------------------------------

PEHLI DAFA — ROOM BANANA
   1. App kholein
   2. Neeche "+ Naya Room banayein" dabayein
   3. Emoji aur rang chunein (taake ek nazar me pehchan ho)
   4. Room ka naam likhein     (misal: "Comedy account")
   5. TikTok username likhein  (misal: @altariq.clips)

   6. Section "1 - VPN":
        - "Select" dabayein, apni VPN app chunein
        - Likh dein kaunsa server  (misal: Dubai - UAE server 3)

   7. Section "2 - Location":
        - Apni Fake GPS app chunein
        - City ka naam likhein
        - Latitude aur Longitude likhein
          (Google Maps me jagah par der tak dabayein, numbers
           copy ho jate hain)

   8. Section "3 - TikTok":
        - TikTok app chunein

   9. Notes me jo chahe likh lein
  10. "Save karein" dabayein

   Har account ke liye yahi dobara karein.


ROZANA KA ISTEMAAL — SIRF 3 TAP
   1. App kholein, jis account par kaam karna hai us room par tap
   2. Step 1 ka button dabayein
        -> aapki VPN app khul jayegi, server aapke saamne likha hai
        -> wapas aayein, step khud tick ho chuka hoga
   3. Step 2 ka button dabayein
        -> coordinates KHUD clipboard par aa jati hain
        -> GPS app khul jayegi, seedha paste karein
   4. Step 3 ka button dabayein
        -> TikTok khul jayega, account switch karein

   Upar "Sequence 2/3" chalta rehta hai, aur jo step baqi ho uska
   card roshan rehta hai — bhoolne ka sawal hi nahi.


VIDEO POST KARNE KE BAAD
   Usi room me neeche "Upload log" me "+ Add" dabayein aur video
   ka naam likh dein. Baad me pata rehta hai kis account par kya
   gaya aur kab.


--------------------------------------------------------
5. FAIDE (BENEFITS)
--------------------------------------------------------

  1. WAQT KI BACHAT
     Har switch par 3-5 minute ki jagah 20-30 second. Din me 5
     baar switch karein to ghante bachte hain.

  2. GHALTI KHATAM
     Har account ki setting apne room me likhi hai. Ek account ki
     location doosre par lagne ka khatra nahi rehta.

  3. KUCH YAAD RAKHNE KI ZAROORAT NAHI
     Kaunsa server, kaunsi city, kaunse coordinates — sab likha
     hua saamne hai.

  4. KUCH DHOONDNA NAHI PARTA
     Phone me apps swipe kar ke talash karne ka jhanjhat khatam.
     Sab kuch ek screen par.

  5. RECORD RAHTA HAI
     Kis account par kab kya post hua — poora hisaab.

  6. NAYE ACCOUNT KA SETUP AASAN
     Naya account? Naya room bana lein, 2 minute ka kaam.

  7. INTERNET KI ZAROORAT NAHI
     App poori tarah offline chalti hai. Koi login nahi, koi
     account banane ka jhanjhat nahi.

  8. AAPKA DATA AAPKE PAAS
     Kuch bhi kisi server par nahi jata. Sab kuch aapke phone ke
     andar rehta hai.


--------------------------------------------------------
6. YE APP KIS KE LIYE HAI
--------------------------------------------------------

  - Content creators jo kai accounts par videos daalte hain
  - Wo log jo alag alag mulk / ilaqe ke audience ke liye alag
    accounts chalate hain
  - Social media manage karne wale jo clients ke accounts
    sambhalte hain
  - Koi bhi jo ek phone par kai accounts ka kaam karta hai aur
    baar baar settings badalte badalte tang aa chuka hai


--------------------------------------------------------
7. ZAROORI BAATEIN
--------------------------------------------------------

  * DATA SIRF PHONE ME HAI
    App offline hai — iska matlab agar aap app uninstall kar dein
    ya phone badal lein, to data chala jayega.

    ISLIYE: upar dayein taraf gear (settings) ka nishan dabayein
    -> "Backup bhejein" -> apne WhatsApp par bhej kar rakh lein.
    Naye phone par wahi text "Backup wapas laayein" me paste kar
    dein, sab wapas aa jayega.

  * APPS PEHLE SE INSTALL HONI CHAHIYEIN
    App sirf wahi apps khol sakti hai jo pehle se aapke phone me
    hain. VPN aur Fake GPS app aap khud install karein.

  * AGAR KOI APP UNINSTALL HO JAYE
    To room me us step par paigham aayega ke app nahi mili — bas
    Edit me jaakar dobara chun lein.
