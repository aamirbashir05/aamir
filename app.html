📋 Ek saath kai entries

Orders aur Payments aik saath paste kar sakte hain — app khud pehchan legi:
Order (Maal): (500 Sticker Sehat KPK)(Flex zone 9500)
Payment (Paisa): 6000 Amjad Press
Bracket wali line = Maal (debit). Number se shuru hone wali = Payment (credit). Har line ka type agli screen par badla bhi ja sakta hai. Poora naam lazmi nahi — "Kohistan Press" bhi "Hamza Kohistan Press" ko dhoond lega.

Uper wala default hai — sirf un lines ke liye jo khud pehchani na jayein.

Paste karne ke baad tag dabayein — har line ke start me wo lag jayega (VC / VC LP / STICKER / UV).
